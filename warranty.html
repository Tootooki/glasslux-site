Lifetime Warranty

Our Guarantee

At GlassLux LLC, we stand behind our workmanship. We offer a Lifetime Warranty on all windshield repairs and replacements for as long as you own the vehicle.

What is Covered?

  • Windshield Repairs: If a repaired chip cracks out from the original damage point, we will credit the cost of the repair toward a full windshield replacement performed by us.
  • Replacements: We warrant against air leaks, water leaks, and molding retention issues due to workmanship for as long as you own the vehicle.

Exclusions

This warranty does not cover damage resulting from vandalism, theft, natural disasters, rust, or new impact damage (new rock chips).

Claims

To make a claim, please contact us with your original invoice.
