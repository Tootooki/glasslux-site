The Science of Auto Glass Repair

Our advanced repair process restores the structural integrity of your windshield while dramatically improving visual clarity. We don't just "fill" the crack; we engineer a bond stronger than the original glass.

  • Vacuum Purge: We remove all air and moisture from the break.
  • Pressure Injection: High-grade optical resin is forced into every micro-fissure.
  • UV Curing: A specialized UV light hardens the resin instantly.
